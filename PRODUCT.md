## Platform

## Users and purpose

FinSight supports analysts who inspect synthetic financial transactions and record investigation outcomes. Admins oversee cases and perform the permitted return and reopen actions. The [product workflows](docs/product-workflows.md) own permissions and lifecycle rules.

## Capabilities and constraints

The discovery workspace provides credential sign-in, role overviews, transaction filters and paging, masked context, recorded risk evidence and analyst case creation. PostgreSQL owns financial facts, sessions and immutable case history. MongoDB telemetry and analyst notes are planned capabilities, not existing panels.

Identifiers remain UUIDs in storage and API calls. Abbreviated typed labels are display hints; full identifiers remain available for exact lookup and copying. Risk signals support review and do not establish a fraud verdict. All demo content must remain labeled synthetic.

## User commitments

The user has accepted the current discovery functionality and requested YouTube-style neutral grey and white themes, a distinct accent, light and dark modes, and square sidebar navigation without the curved left active marker. The sidebar must remain. The user accepted this visual direction and authorized pushing it on 2026-10-27.

## Evidence and accessibility

The [discovery workspace](docs/discovery-ui.md) records routes and integration boundaries. The [development setup](docs/development-setup.md) owns runnable checks. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, masked personal data and responsive tables remain design requirements. The course requirements PDF is local and must not be redistributed.
